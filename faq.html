Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ISAI Art Cafe.

What are your opening hours?
We are open everyday starting from 5:00 PM until 11:00 PM. We recommend booking a slot in advance for weekends!
Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?
Yes! While our signature dishes include meat and dairy, we offer several delicious vegan and gluten-free alternatives upon request. Just ask your server.
Can I host a private event or workshop here?
Absolutely! ISAI is the perfect venue for creative workshops, intimate gatherings, and acoustic sessions. Please contact us via WhatsApp or our Contact page to discuss your event.
Is there parking available?
We have limited parking right in front of the cafe, but there is plenty of street parking available on Anna Salai Road in Palavakkam.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, we are a pet-friendly cafe! We just ask that your furry friends remain well-behaved and stay in the designated outdoor/patio seating areas.
