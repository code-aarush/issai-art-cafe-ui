Our Story

Nestled in the heart of Palavakkam, Chennai, ISAI is more than just a café—it's a sanctuary where creativity meets comfort.

Founded on the belief that art and food are universal languages, we've created a cozy haven for artists, dreamers, and coffee enthusiasts alike. Every corner of our space is curated to inspire, from the local artworks adorning our walls to the meticulously crafted dishes on our menu.
